## 0. What changed my priors while doing this

Three things that were not in the programme's own record:

1. **The source papers of two of our four main surfaces already publish the reliability numbers F1 and F6 report.**
   X-Atlas/Orion's own preprint says 48.36 % (HCT116) / 40.37 % (HEK293T) / 54.30 % (K562) of on-target perturbations
   in expressed genes show *"discernible single-cell transcriptomic changes"* [ORION]; Replogle 2022 reports
   *"2,987 of 9,608 genetic perturbations targeting a primary transcript (31.1%)"* with a significant transcriptional
   phenotype and 30.5 % clearing a > 10-DEG bar [REPLOGLE22]. **Our F6's 85/272 = 31 % of panel targets at or above the
   10-gene gate is, to the digit, Replogle's own published 30.5 %.**
2. **A 2026 preprint has already made F1's central claim at scale.** Wang, Kuipers, Hugi, Platt & Beerenwinkel,
   *Reliable single-cell perturbations explain and improve model performance* (bioRxiv, 2026-08-11): *"Among 7,170
   perturbations from 29 datasets, 65% are unreliable, 11% shared, and 24% specific."* Their reliability is split-half
   Pearson with a Spearman–Brown correction — the same construction as ours, on a superset of our datasets — and they
   publish the depth law we retracted a version of: *"84% unreliable at <50 cells; 10% at >500 cells"* [WANG26].
3. **The Broad-16 source paper explains C2a's failure and partly contradicts F5's headline.** Maffa et al.,
   *Dissecting context-dependent cancer vulnerabilities using Perturb-seq* (bioRxiv, 2026-08-24) report that the
   *"strongest predictor of Perturb-seq statistical power was the total number of UMIs recovered"* (r = 0.85) with an
   elbow near 5 M UMIs per perturbation — which is exactly 283's post-hoc depth explanation, already published. And they
   *do* predict line-specific responses from cell state: p53 activation on UBE3A/MDM2 only in the HPV-E6 lines SKGII and
   C4I, and IER3IP1 dependence from a hemizygous 18q/11q co-deletion (Mann–Whitney p = 3.28 × 10⁻⁶), *"synthetic
   lethality between co-deletion"* [BROAD16].

## 2. What the write-up can claim as new, and with what caveats

**Tier 1 — defensible as new, with caveats stated in the same sentence.**

1. **Rank-retrieval needs the noise-dominated directions of its own whitening operator (F3b).** The novel content is
   that damping the directions where an independent noise estimate exceeds the fitted eigenvalue — the standard
   shrinkage-whitening prescription — *loses* retrieval on 3/3 folds at every κ, and that the properly cross-fitted
   operator is neutral. Caveats: one operator family, one metric, three folds; the mechanism (target-specific signal is
   5–10 % of row energy, spread over exactly those directions) is inferred, not directly measured; and the eigenvalue-bias
   half of the story is textbook and must be cited as such ([LEDOIT04], [RMT-SC]).
2. **Cross-line conservation does not predict within-individual state dependence, though it does predict the
   across-donor ceiling (F11c).** Preregistered, and the preregistered index was retracted honestly. Caveats: 74 targets,
   ~66–107 cells per side, one cell type, and the deconfounded ρ interval covers zero, so the claim is "no relation
   detected", not "no relation".
3. **The donor is a marginal replicate unit relative to cells, and replicates beat cells at a matched budget in CD4
   T cells (F10b).** Caveats: 4 donors, one condition, a guide-split comparator, and a live tension with [IPSC34], which
   finds heritable donor modulation of trans effects at 26 donors.
4. **A family-resolved cross-line conservation axis, preregistered and magnitude/reliability-controlled (F9).** New as a
   *quantification*, not as a direction — the core-machinery-vs-cell-identity ordering is what the co-essentiality and
   lineage-dependency literature expects. Strengthened by the independent reproduction inside [GWCD4i-TAB]'s own table
   (AUC 0.58, p 0.013–0.019, interval clearing zero in 1 of 3 conditions). Hold F12a's +0.811 until 284 lands.

**Tier 2 — publishable only as re-measurement, explicitly crediting the source.**

5. **F1's reliability law** must be framed as "we reproduce, on the challenge's own panel and with a residual-cosine
   estimator, the published result that most perturbation responses in genome-scale screens are not reproducible"
   ([ORION], [REPLOGLE22], [WANG26], [PAN26]) — and must distinguish itself from Orion's NTC-profile Spearman of 0.99.
6. **F6's DE sparsity** is Replogle's own 30.5 %. Publishable as a *scorer-faithful* re-derivation (the challenge's
   Wilcoxon-BH recipe, matched controls) plus the [NADIG25] caveat that DEG counts understate the true effect.
7. **F5a's decomposition** is new in form; its two named programmes are the source paper's own.
8. **F2's retained fraction** is a good methodological contribution provided it is framed against circular-analysis prior
   art and explicitly separated from [WANG26]'s positive reliability-based *training* selection.

**Do not claim as scientific findings:** F7 (private-scorer calibration), C1 (a robustness check), F11a's ratio
(interval covers zero), F10c (contradicted and retracted), and F4a's knee *as a discovery* (largely a property of the
decoy distribution).

**A structural point worth making in the paper, which no single finding states:** the programme measured three different
"reproducibility" quantities and the literature conflates them — (i) within-reference split-cell repeatability (what the
challenge's score-1 anchor is), (ii) across-replicate/across-donor agreement (what [GWCD4i-TAB] publishes), and
(iii) across-context transfer (what [TRANSPORT26] formalises). F1/F10/F11 together show these are numerically far apart
on the same cells (+0.243, +0.054, +0.03 on the challenge panel). That separation, stated cleanly, is a more useful
contribution than any single number here.

## 3. Contradictions and tensions to resolve before publication

**C-1 (hard). F5's headline "not basal-state-predictable" vs [BROAD16]'s own state-based predictions.** They predict
line-specific responses from HPV-E6 status and from an arm-level co-deletion; we report a null for a linear ridge on two
basal expression programmes. *Settle on disk:* re-run 268's LOLO with lineage, HPV status and arm-level copy number from
`data/external/ccle_broad_2025` / `data/external/depmap26q1` as the state features. Until then the headline must be
narrowed to "not predictable from basal *expression* by a linear model at 11 training lines".

**C-2 (hard). F10's surviving sentence "cells are not the binding constraint at any genome-scale budget"
(`docs/FINDINGS-2026-09.md:302`).** Contradicted by [WANG26]'s measured 84 % → 10 % unreliable law over cell count and
by [BROAD16]'s UMI–power relation (r = 0.85, elbow ≈ 5 M UMIs/perturbation); already retracted in the same finding's
Measurement paragraph, and `docs/READ-FIRST.md:69` states the opposite ("the depth axis is OPEN pending 285"), so the
two project documents disagree. *Settle:* delete the sentence now; 285 supplies the replacement on cell-level data.

**T1 (tension, not a contradiction). F3b vs shrinkage-regularised whitening.** The embedding-retrieval and covariance
literature recommends Soft-ZCA / Ledoit–Wolf-regularised whitening and a 90–95 % variance-retention region; we measure
that any damping loses `pds`. Different objective (300-way scale-invariant rank retrieval vs semantic similarity /
classification) and different geometry (target signal in the low-variance directions). *Settle:* state the objective
difference, and report the one comparison that is like-for-like — the soft floor c = 1 is null on `pds` and **+4.4 %
`cos_agg`**, i.e. the standard prescription helps the similarity objective and not the retrieval one, which is exactly
what the literature's task-dependence caveat predicts.

**T2 (tension). F10b's marginal donor term vs [IPSC34]'s heritable donor modulation.** Different cell type (CD4 T cells
vs iPSCs), n (4 vs 26 donors), and statistic (residual cosine vs trans-effect magnitude with eQTL links). Not resolvable
on disk; name it.

**T3 (confound). F8's conservation score and response magnitude.** In [GWCD4i-TAB]'s own table, conservation tracks the
MASH DE-gene count at ρ +0.54 — five times its correlation with the authors' own cross-donor reproducibility column
(+0.11…+0.17). F9 reports a magnitude-matched contrast; F8 does not. *Settle on disk:* recompute F8's LOLO prediction of
per-target `pds` within magnitude strata using `E276/work/conservation276.csv` and the existing DE-count column.

**T4 (procedure). C2a's threshold was not depth-conditioned.** [BROAD16] had already published the UMI–power law before
the reserve was frozen. The reserve is spent; the lesson binds the next preregistration on any multi-line set: stratify
or covariate-adjust the pass rule on sequencing depth per perturbation.

**T5 (framing). F2 vs [WANG26].** "Selection cannot precede estimation" reads as contradicting a published positive
result (reliability-selected training perturbations matching full data with 55 % of the rows). Fix by naming the
operation: F2 rejects *per-target selection of a donor row by proximity to the truth*, not *reliability-based filtering
of training data*.
