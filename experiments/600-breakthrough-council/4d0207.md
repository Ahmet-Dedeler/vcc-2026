## 3. Mac vs cloud: is the MacBook enough?

- **The Mac is idle most of the time.** Load average is about 3 of 18 cores, and the chains sleep until 20:00Z. The
  research rate is not FLOP-limited. It is limited by 2 board reads a day, by transfer validity, and by ideas.
- **The Mac's real limit is memory, not CPU.** A full `cell-eval2` takes about 25 GB RSS and is single-threaded (221: 13.7 %
  of an 8-vCPU box), and a build takes 12–20 GB. With Ahmet's apps open the Mac holds 26–29 GiB free+inactive, so **one
  heavy job at a time**. A six-member real-scorer read takes about 2.5 h, so 10 reads take a day locally.
- **The cloud wins in exactly two cases:**
  1. **Many real-scorer reads at once.** `scripts/cloud_score.sh` exists and was validated (221: $1.48 per six-member
     score; its known-answer row reproduces 095's h1-test numbers; three self-termination guards). Twenty reads in
     parallel take about 3 h and $30, against about 2 days serially here.
  2. **GPU training** of a learned cross-line model, if the biology territory proposes one. A g6e/L40S-class instance
     costs about $2/h. Small models train fine on the Mac's MPS.
- Everything else stays on the Mac: builds, def_day, slots, and anything touching gitignored data that has not been
  staged to S3.
- **Recommendation:** use the cloud as a scorer farm and a GPU box **on demand for the portfolio**, not as a standing
  cluster. Each run is launched through `cloud_score.sh`, which terminates itself, and `aws_reap.sh` runs afterwards.

## 4. Hypotheses from this territory

### H-600a — a real-scorer farm for every October switch (instrument; class: instrument, protects transfer)
- **Mechanism.** October switches are priced through proxies. The H1 → board ratio per lever class spans 0.07–2.3×
  (`docs/H1-BOARD-CALIBRATION.md`), and H1 mis-signs carrier nmae. A wrong rider costs about 0.001–0.005 on D/E/F, with no
  board to catch it. Scoring each pending switch with the REAL six-member scorer on K held-out surfaces in parallel turns
  one proxy read into K real reads.
- **Members.** All six, read directly.
- **Expected gain.** No score by itself. It protects about 0.003–0.01 of D/E/F score by vetoing non-transferring riders,
  and it tells us which of the 10-05 … 10-07 switches are real.
- **Cheapest decisive test.** A cloud retrodiction of two known board pairs on H1: 446bj2z vs 446bj2 (zs; board nmae
  −0.00218 raw) and 479p2g vs 446bj2 (P2g). Four scores, about $6, about 3 h wall clock, no local memory. It passes if
  the cloud reproduces the local H1 numbers to 1e-6 and ranks both pairs the board's way.
- **Cost.** About $1.5 per score. 10 switches × 6 surfaces is about $90 of credit and about 3 h wall clock.

### H-600b — on-demand GPU for whichever learned model the biology territory proposes (compute enabler)
