## 5. Hypotheses (ranked)

**H-610a: a DE-channel sprint before Oct 22 is the highest-value use of the remaining slots.**
- Mechanism: the pack's DE designs beat ours by +0.025 … +0.041 overall using public data. H1 mis-prices DE design
  (0.07–0.67× on fid, sign errors on nmae), so our H1-optimised DE channel is probably H1-overfit. Only the board reads
  DE truthfully, and it goes dark on Oct 22, which leaves about 38 reads.
- Members: fid, reach, nmae. jac is held.
- Class: scorer mechanics, plus transfer where the sign source changes.
- Test: DE-only arms with the bulk pinned. pds/mse then reproduce to 16 significant figures, so the read is the DE change
  plus a ~0.0003 re-roll (548). Use preregistered pairs and dose curves.
- Cost: slots now, at no data cost.
- First candidates: (i) the 10-06 K16 and 10-07 K20 reads already scheduled; (ii) reach ordering by transferred public
  significance (Chiu S1-style), priced first on PLOLO/H1 at fixed calls; (iii) a pure-nmae step on uncalled gate genes
  (cqawesome 09-18 signature), whose dose is d4cd5f's.

**H-610b: the fid gap is filler SIGN precision, not size and not composition.**
- Evidence: §2. The size curves are flat, null skew is worthless, and sign-confidence filler costs jac. Pure-fid rival
  steps touched only non-DE calls. On h1-test the context's own common response gives +0.03 fid over CLEAN4 on the
  same block.
- Gain: +0.014 raw (+0.008 overall) is demonstrated by the pack; the h1-test oracle ceiling is ~+0.03 raw (+0.017
  overall).
- Class: TRANSFER. It needs a predictor of a NEW line's any-knockdown response. 441's public routes failed: controls
  +0.0055, identity axis +0.0014, Arc H1 HARM. The pack's route is unknown. The candidates are model-signed filler from
  multi-source transfer that keeps the shared component (AtlasShift-lineage designs subtract it, while William's v1
  modelled it as "K562 shared response × DepMap ratio"), or more lines in the common-response average.
- Test, local and cheap (< 4 GB, about 1–2 h): leave-one-line-out over the donor banks on disk (K562 gwps/ess, Jurkat,
  HepG2, RPE1, HCT116, HEK293T, kolf2, H1). For each held-out line, score the non-DE sign precision of each candidate
  common-response predictor on that line's CPM block: CLEAN4, a mean over N lines, shared-component estimates, and
  model-signed filler. A board arm is worth building only if one beats CLEAN4 by ≥ +0.01 on most folds. Owner: 7772dc
  or d4cd5f (DE sign source).
- Strongest concrete candidate: chill's day-1 design, a supervised DE model trained on source studies with
  family-balanced weights. It has separate heads for membership P(DE | target, gene, context features) and direction
  P(up | DE, ...), and it emits the counts to realise those calls. On the board it gives fid 0.5225 and nmae 0.835 from
  day 1 with public data. Test it as a leave-one-line-out model on the banks, scored on the DE members with the real
  emitter.

**H-610c: the D/E/F race is for second place, so put October effort where the pack is.**
- Illumina's information edge cannot be matched with public data. cqawesome/Testh/chill hold a DE-design edge that
  carries.
- Implication: for the final recipe, a DE redesign that closes even half the pack gap (+0.012 … +0.02) is worth more than
  any remaining bulk rider (≤ +0.003 each).
- Class: strategy. Test: none. It re-weights the portfolio.

**H-610d (minor): guide-sequence effects are the one lever that is invariant across D/E/F by construction.**
- Arc's blog: "same sgRNA sequences in all six lines". F16 (306–310) measured promoter seed-match repression of ~7.6 %
  on about 1 gene per construct.
- At best this is a reach rider (a sure-sign gene at the head of a small pool). It needs the 2026 library's guide

- Low priority unless the library can be identified.
