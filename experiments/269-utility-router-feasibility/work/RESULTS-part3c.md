## 9. Failure analysis — where the router acts and loses, and whether ANY feature finds the targets the oracle gains on (`work/model269.log`, `work/model269.json` `failure_analysis`)

**(i) Acted-and-lost rows, τ 0.02.** Setting C, ridge: 75 actions on the three cancer contexts, 15 won (Σ +2.38 `pds`), 30 lost (Σ −2.31), the rest exactly 0; Spearman(predicted gain, realised) **−0.20** on the acted rows — the ridge's confidence is anti-informative where it acts. By action: `choose HCT116` 43 actions, mean −0.027, 51 % losses; `choose HEK293T` 24, +0.029, 29 %; `choose K562` 4, +0.062; `undeflate` 4, +0.074. Setting C, GBM: 371 actions, 126 won (Σ +12.0), 177 lost (Σ −19.6); Spearman(pred, realised) +0.05; every action class loses on average (`choose HCT116` −0.016 on 133, `choose HEK293T` −0.037 on 86, `choose K562` −0.022 on 74, `undeflate` −0.009 on 78; loss fractions 0.38–0.52). Setting A reads the same (ridge 84 actions, 17 won / 33 lost, Spearman −0.02; GBM 363, 125 / 164). **Which features drove the lost actions** (AUC "lost row has the higher value", C / ridge, then C / GBM): `s_resid_cos_base` 0.22 / 0.36, `s_cd4_deflated_cos_base` 0.24 / 0.36, `s_cd4_cos_base` 0.26 / 0.36, `s_cd4_de_log` 0.24 / 0.43, `s_dd_wht` 0.28 / 0.41, `s_resid_norm` 0.29 / 0.43 — the router acts, and loses, on the targets whose donors and residuals disagree with the base and whose CD4 effect is weak: it has learned that the unreliable targets are where the oracle's gains sit (item ii) and switches donor there, where the switch is a coin flip that costs twice what it earns (263 §5). Lost rows are LESS often in 262's group (e) than won rows (0.10 vs 0.27 ridge; 0.21 vs 0.33 GBM): the losses are on the marginal band, where a wrong switch pushes a target that was just above the threshold below it.

**(ii) The targets the oracle gains most on (top decile of the labelled oracle within each context: 86 of 853 rows, 69 % in group (e); their best action is split evenly — `choose HEK293T` 25, `choose K562` 25, `choose HCT116` 21, `undeflate` 15).** Per-feature AUC (top-decile target has the higher value), with a permutation reference for the largest |AUC − 0.5| over the 40 features (300 permutations of the top-decile flag within context; 95th percentile **0.176**):

**Five observable features DO find the targets that matter** — the ones whose donors disagree with each other and whose residuals disagree with the base (262's anti-aligned group seen through observables: group (e) itself has AUC 0.26 on `s_dd_wht`, 0.32 on `s_resid_cos_base`; the DepMap in-line effect and pan-essentiality reach only 0.59). But **finding them is not knowing what to do with them**: among the top-decile targets the best action is one of four with near-equal frequency, and the same features have AUC 0.22–0.41 for "the action LOST" among the acted rows — the router that acts on them loses. Target biology (co-essentiality PCs, regulon membership, essentiality in the recipient) separates neither the high-gain targets nor the right action (AUC within ±0.06 of chance), and the recipient's basal programmes are fold-constant and cannot rank targets within a context. This is the scientific content of the negative: **unreliability is observable, the correct alternative is not** — the same asymmetry 263 measured for its arbiters (coin flips against the oracle's pick) and 262 for its selectors (the below-chance group cannot be found below `pds` 0.685), now with a learned model and 50 features.
