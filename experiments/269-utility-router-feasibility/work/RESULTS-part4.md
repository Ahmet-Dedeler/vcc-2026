## 10. Honest limits

- **Labels are per-target arrays of whole-panel arms, not single-target switches.** 265 measured the non-additivity; here it is small at the label level (across-arm SD of a (t, j) label median 0.0008; proxy totals within 0.001 of every real arm total) and the labels average 11–47 arms each, but a label is still "this target's `pds` when many rows are switched", and a production router that switches few rows would realise a slightly different number per target. The decision-level totals inherit ≤ 0.0015 of proxy error per arm (265's bound).
- **One instrument, three cancer lines and an iPSC line.** The oracle ceilings (+0.046 realised, +0.065 labelled) are PLOLO ceilings on deep pseudobulk truths with held operators; their board transfer for a base re-direction is unmeasured (263 §10). Every model reading here is negative or null before any factor is applied, so the verdict does not depend on a transfer factor.
- **Leave-one-context-out with three test contexts** has three folds; a stratified 3-context bootstrap resamples targets, not lines — a line-level σ does not exist on this data (the programme's rule 1: the unit is named, and it is the target within a context).
- **The clean held context is an iPSC line.** KOLF2 is the only line whose response is a donor nowhere, so it is the only leakage-free test of target-level transfer on disk; the held-line-free construction on the cancer folds (§5 T3) is its cancer-line counterpart but uses 264's matched three-donor rows (KOLF2 at matched amplitude inside the candidate rows), i.e. a different action set whose ceiling (+0.053) is comparable to 263's. A held-line-free two-donor test on the cancer lines needs training folds re-staged WITHOUT the held line among the donors (one operator load per fold, ≈ 5.4 GiB, 263's staging) — not done here (time box; it is the stage-2 instrument if anything reopens).
- **HEK293T and KOLF2 are not in DepMap's gene-effect matrix**; the recipient-side DepMap feature is missing on those contexts (flagged). DepMap 24Q2 was used (the 26Q1 name on disk is the Broad-16 set); families and PCs would move slightly with another release.
- **Model capacity was capped by the mandate** (≤ 50 features, depth ≤ 3); a larger model on the same 1,554 held rows would have the same information and more variance. The GBM already reproduces its shuffled controls, i.e. it fits marginals, not targets.
- **Norm matching is 263's construction**, shape-only on PLOLO; a production form would have to reproduce it. No production form exists and none is proposed.
- 066's nested gate was not re-run (different arms); its qualitative result (a same-target lookup does not transfer between cancer lines) is what §5 T3 confirms in the donor-choice action space.
