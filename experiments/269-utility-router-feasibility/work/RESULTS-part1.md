## 1. Labels — the frozen action set, per (target, context) (`build269.py` → `work/rows_choose269.csv`, `work/rows_defl269.csv`)

**Unit.** The row is a (target, context) pair; the context is a held cell line of 162's PLOLO (`k562_gwps` = K562, `orion_hct116` = HCT116, `orion_hek293t` = HEK293T, `kolf2` = KOLF2); a fold's "truth" is that line's deep pseudobulk response, its "donors" the other production lines' rows. Every σ, interval and p below resamples or permutes **targets within a context** (paired target bootstrap, 20,000 resamples; stratified over the three cancer contexts for the 3-context mean); nothing here resamples cells, guides or batches.

**Actions.** `keep` (u₀ = 0: the incumbent `cd4svd5` row, the equal-weight whitened donor mean + the deflated CD4 and H1-union residuals); `choose K562` / `choose HCT116` / `choose HEK293T` (replace the donor mean by that donor's un-whitened row, norm-matched to the mean row's norm before whitening — 263's `:nm` construction, shape-only; available where that line is a donor of the context and carries the target); `undeflate` (switch the CD4 deflation off for that target: the `h1union` row instead of the `cd4svd5` row).

**Label construction and the additivity caveat.** Per-target `pds` is the scorer's rank statistic (`resid218._per_target`, tag a23, mean over frames A/B/C). 265 measured that it is not row-local through the pipeline (one global preserve scalar + a nonlinear render), so no saved arm gives "target t alone switched". The labels are therefore read off the saved per-target arrays as the mandate instructs: **`u(choose j, t) = mean over every 263 norm-matched selection arm whose pick for t was donor j of [pds_arm(t) − pds_cd4svd5(t)]`** — 87 such arms per cancer fold (7 arbiters × {raw, wht} × {pick, reverse} × 3 gates + 9 random picks; 75 on `kolf2`), the oracle, softmax and majority arms EXCLUDED so that no label depends on a truth-derived pick of another target. Each (t, j) label averages a median of 26 arms (5–95 %: 11–39); the across-arm SD of the same (t, j) label has median **0.0008** (95th percentile 0.0047) — the context of the other switched rows barely moves a target's own `pds`. **`u(undeflate, t) = pds_h1union(t) − pds_cd4svd5(t)`** from 262's `diag262-<fold>.npz`. 265-style proxy check: each real arm's total, recomputed from leave-that-arm-out labels, reproduces its real total with error mean −0.00004 … +0.00002, SD 0.0001–0.0004, max |error| 0.0010 (cancer) / 0.0015 (`kolf2`) across all 87 / 75 arms — the labels are additive to the level 265 required (< 0.3 of a null SD).

## 3. Splits, models, decision rule, metric (`model269.py`)

- **(A) leave-one-CONTEXT-out** over the three cancer contexts: train on the other two cancer contexts + KOLF2, test on the held one (3 folds; the held context's targets ARE in training through the other contexts — the setting that tests target-level transfer).
- **(B) held TARGET FAMILIES**: for each family, train on the other nine families' rows from all four contexts, test on the family's rows in the three cancer contexts (10 folds; contexts are seen).
- **(C) both**: for each (cancer context, family), train on rows with a different context AND a different family, test on that context's rows of that family (30 folds; every cancer row is predicted exactly once by a model that saw neither its line nor its family). **The honest number.**
- Fitted inside every split: DepMap PCs (training targets), basal PCs (training lines), imputation/standardisation (training rows), ridge α by 5-fold GroupKFold over targets on the training rows (grid 0.3–1000).
- **Models**: ridge (choose rows pooled over donors with donor-relative features + identity; deflation rows with the shared features) and a shallow gradient-boosted regressor (`HistGradientBoostingRegressor`, depth 3, 300 trees, lr 0.05, min leaf 20, ℓ₂ 1). **Decision per (context, target)**: the action with the highest predicted utility among the available ones; act if that prediction exceeds τ, else keep. **Metric**: the decision-level Δ`pds` = mean over ALL targets of the held context of the realised label of the chosen action (0 where it keeps) — the fold's Δ`pds` in 162's units — with the paired target bootstrap; the fraction of the labelled oracle (and of 263's realised oracle) captured; acted fraction; precision (acted rows with a positive realised label); pick accuracy vs the labelled best action (reference only).
- **Baselines**: keep; every fixed action; the two oracles; **shuffled features** (feature rows permuted within (context, donor), train and test; 3 seeds); **shuffled target labels** (training labels permuted within (context, donor); 3 seeds); random action at the model's acting rate (3 seeds); **target-only lookup** (066's same-target form: the held target's best mean utility over the training contexts; setting A only, where the target is seen); **recipient-only** (the fixed action best on the training contexts). 066's nested gate is not reproducible in this action space (its arms were 064's PerturbMatch / CD4 arms, not donor choices) and is not re-run.

The reporting margin is τ = 0.02 (the `pds` band's knee, 262 §4.2); the whole curve is in §4.
