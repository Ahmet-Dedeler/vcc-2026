## 1. Labels — the frozen action set, per (target, context) (`build269.py` → `work/rows_choose269.csv`, `work/rows_defl269.csv`)

**Unit.** The row is a (target, context) pair; the context is a held cell line of 162's PLOLO (`k562_gwps` = K562, `orion_hct116` = HCT116, `orion_hek293t` = HEK293T, `kolf2` = KOLF2); a fold's "truth" is that line's deep pseudobulk response, its "donors" the other production lines' rows. Every σ, interval and p below resamples or permutes **targets within a context** (paired target bootstrap, 20,000 resamples; stratified over the three cancer contexts for the 3-context mean); nothing here resamples cells, guides or batches.

**Actions.** `keep` (u₀ = 0: the incumbent `cd4svd5` row, the equal-weight whitened donor mean + the deflated CD4 and H1-union residuals); `choose K562` / `choose HCT116` / `choose HEK293T` (replace the donor mean by that donor's un-whitened row, norm-matched to the mean row's norm before whitening — 263's `:nm` construction, shape-only; available where that line is a donor of the context and carries the target); `undeflate` (switch the CD4 deflation off for that target: the `h1union` row instead of the `cd4svd5` row).

**Label construction and the additivity caveat.** Per-target `pds` is the scorer's rank statistic (`resid218._per_target`, tag a23, mean over frames A/B/C). 265 measured that it is not row-local through the pipeline (one global preserve scalar + a nonlinear render), so no saved arm gives "target t alone switched". The labels are therefore read off the saved per-target arrays as the mandate instructs: **`u(choose j, t) = mean over every 263 norm-matched selection arm whose pick for t was donor j of [pds_arm(t) − pds_cd4svd5(t)]`** — 87 such arms per cancer fold (six two-space arbiters × {raw, wht} × {pick, reverse} × 3 gates = 72, the basal arbiter × {pick, reverse} × 3 gates = 6, and 9 random picks; 75 on `kolf2`, which has no `kolf2` arbiter), the oracle, softmax and majority arms EXCLUDED so that no label depends on a truth-derived pick of another target. ## 3. Splits, models, decision rule, metric (`model269.py`)

- **(A) leave-one-CONTEXT-out** over the three cancer contexts: train on the other two cancer contexts + KOLF2, test on the held one (3 folds; the held context's targets ARE in training through the other contexts — the setting that tests target-level transfer).
- **(B) held TARGET FAMILIES**: for each family, train on the other nine families' rows from all four contexts, test on the family's rows in the three cancer contexts (10 folds; contexts are seen).
- **(C) both**: for each (cancer context, family), train on rows with a different context AND a different family, test on that context's rows of that family (30 folds; every cancer row is predicted exactly once by a model that saw neither its line nor its family). **The honest number.**
- Fitted inside every split: DepMap PCs (training targets), basal PCs (training lines), imputation/standardisation (training rows), ridge α by 5-fold GroupKFold over targets on the training rows (grid 0.3–1000; the CV chose 1000, the top of the grid, in every split of every setting — the data ask for the most-shrunk model offered, so the ridge is close to a constant predictor and its rare actions come from the donor-identity / context means).
- **Models**: ridge (choose rows pooled over donors with donor-relative features + identity; deflation rows with the shared features) and a shallow gradient-boosted regressor (`HistGradientBoostingRegressor`, depth 3, 300 trees, lr 0.05, min leaf 20, ℓ₂ 1). **Decision per (context, target)**: the action with the highest predicted utility among the available ones; act if that prediction exceeds τ, else keep. **Metric**: the decision-level Δ`pds` = mean over ALL targets of the held context of the realised label of the chosen action (0 where it keeps) — the fold's Δ`pds` in 162's units — with the paired target bootstrap; the fraction of the labelled oracle (and of 263's realised oracle) captured; acted fraction; precision (acted rows with a positive realised label); pick accuracy vs the labelled best action (reference only).
- **Baselines**: keep; every fixed action; the two oracles; **shuffled features** (feature rows permuted within (context, donor), train and test; 3 seeds); **shuffled target labels** (training labels permuted within (context, donor); 3 seeds); random action at the model's acting rate (3 seeds); **target-only lookup** (066's same-target form: the held target's best mean utility over the training contexts; setting A only, where the target is seen); **recipient-only** (the fixed action best on the training contexts). 066's nested gate is not reproducible in this action space (its arms were 064's PerturbMatch / CD4 arms, not donor choices) and is not re-run.

The reporting margin is τ = 0.02 (the `pds` band's knee, 262 §4.2); the whole curve is in §4.

## 10. Honest limits

- **Labels are per-target arrays of whole-panel arms, not single-target switches.** 265 measured the non-additivity; here it is small at the label level (across-arm SD of a (t, j) label median 0.0008; proxy totals within 0.001 of every real arm total) and the labels average 11–47 arms each, but a label is still "this target's `pds` when many rows are switched", and a production router that switches few rows would realise a slightly different number per target. The decision-level totals inherit ≤ 0.0015 of proxy error per arm (265's bound).
- **One instrument, three cancer lines and an iPSC line.** The oracle ceilings (+0.046 realised, +0.065 labelled) are PLOLO ceilings on deep pseudobulk truths with held operators; their board transfer for a base re-direction is unmeasured (263 §10). Every model reading here is negative or null before any factor is applied, so the verdict does not depend on a transfer factor.
- **Leave-one-context-out with three test contexts** has three folds; a stratified 3-context bootstrap resamples targets, not lines — a line-level σ does not exist on this data (the programme's rule 1: the unit is named, and it is the target within a context).
- **The clean held context is an iPSC line.** KOLF2 is the only line whose response is a donor nowhere, so it is the only leakage-free test of target-level transfer on disk; the held-line-free construction on the cancer folds (§5 T3) is its cancer-line counterpart but uses 264's matched three-donor rows (KOLF2 at matched amplitude inside the candidate rows), i.e. a different action set whose ceiling (+0.053) is comparable to 263's. A held-line-free two-donor test on the cancer lines needs training folds re-staged WITHOUT the held line among the donors (one operator load per fold, ≈ 5.4 GiB, 263's staging) — not done here (time box; it is the stage-2 instrument if anything reopens).
- **HEK293T and KOLF2 are not in DepMap's gene-effect matrix**; the recipient-side DepMap feature is missing on those contexts (flagged). DepMap 24Q2 was used (the 26Q1 name on disk is the Broad-16 set); families and PCs would move slightly with another release.
- **Model capacity was capped by the mandate** (≤ 50 features, depth ≤ 3); a larger model on the same 1,554 held rows would have the same information and more variance. The GBM already reproduces its shuffled controls, i.e. it fits marginals, not targets.
- **Norm matching is 263's construction**, shape-only on PLOLO; a production form would have to reproduce it. No production form exists and none is proposed.
- 066's nested gate was not re-run (different arms); its qualitative result (a same-target lookup does not transfer between cancer lines) is what §5 T3 confirms in the donor-choice action space.
