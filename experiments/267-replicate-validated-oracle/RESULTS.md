## 6. The three-outcome reading

- **Survives?** No. Cross-validated, the oracle is below the mean on 3/3 folds in both directions and on both split kinds at gate all (−0.009…−0.032 per fold), within noise of zero at τ0 (−0.001, +0.000), and its retained fraction is negative or zero in all four cells (−0.32, −0.03, −0.25, +0.01). The abstaining oracle, 263's largest ceiling, cross-validates to −0.010.
- **Disappears?** Yes, in the precise sense the mandate asked: the +0.046 is selection against the reference's own noise. The in-sample oracle on a half (+0.056) EXCEEDS the deep-truth oracle (+0.046) because a noisier truth offers more to select; the deep-truth pick keeps ~55–60 % of it on a half it half-contains; independent halves keep none. What the picks share with each other (53–59 % agreement) is what a coin shares with itself.
- **Class-specific?** Only in the weak form: in the reliable third of the panel the pick carries information (+0.021…+0.027 over a coin flip, replicated on both splits) but ties the mean there; in 262's anti-aligned group — 48 % of the in-sample gain, the group 263 pointed at — the cross-validated pick equals a random pick (+0.038 vs +0.040) and the group is the least reproducible tier of the reference (median reliability 0.014–0.036). So the biological hypothesis this leaves is not "some targets prefer one donor line" but "**the below-chance targets are the ones whose response is not measured at 150–200 cells**": their truth is a noise direction, the base's cosine to it is a coin flip, and nothing chosen from the donors can be validated against it.

## 7. Honest limits

- **One instrument, three cancer lines, batch- or cell-level halves.** The halves are independent samples of the SAME experiment (same library, same lab, same day-structure); they resample batches or cells, never studies. A truth that reproduces across halves could still be study-specific, and a truth that does not reproduce across halves cannot be rescued by another study — the negative direction of this result is the robust one.
- **The reliability numbers depend on the centring only through the shared component**, and the three centrings agree; they do depend on depth (median 70–95 cells per half), which is half the bank's depth. Spearman–Brown to full depth is an extrapolation (159 §1.3 measured its error at 4–14 %).
- **The oracle is 263's rule verbatim** (whitened-row cosine, argmax, norm-matched); a selector that uses the truth differently (soft weights, thresholds) was not re-screened — 263 showed softmax weighting tracks the hard pick, and the abstaining oracle, the largest ceiling, is cross-validated here and negative.
- **The guide split is 8–10 targets per fold**; its intervals are ±0.05–0.10 and it can only rule out large guide-specific effects.
- **Multiplicity**: 4 oracle cells × 10 arms, 12 strata × 2 splits, 3 guide cells. Every headline negative is 3/3 folds and both split kinds; the one positive stratum (T3, cross − random) reads p 0.03 over the 12 batch-split strata (0.06 over all 24 cells) and replicates at 4.1 σ on the random split.
- **Memory**: the Orion passes exceeded the mandate's ~10 GB by 1–3 GB (peaks 11.0 / 13.0 GiB; the truth builders 11.6 / 11.3). The machine stayed at ≥ 45 % free throughout; no sibling job was affected as far as `memory_pressure` shows. The intermediate sums (`work/half/<line>/acc_*.npy`, 10.6 GB) are kept because re-extraction costs 42 min; delete them if space matters.
