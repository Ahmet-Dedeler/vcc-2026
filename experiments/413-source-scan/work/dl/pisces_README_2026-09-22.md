# X-Atlas/Pisces

X-Atlas/Pisces is a Perturb-seq atlas containing 25.6 million perturbed single-cell transcriptomes across 16 biologically diverse contexts, including widely used cell lines (HCT116, HEK293T, HepG2),
induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs), resting and CD3/CD28 activated Jurkat T lymphoma cells, and multi-lineage differentiating iPSCs.
