## 5. Honest limits

- **The noise covariance is estimated on ~270–290 panel rows per donor** (the rows with two halves), projected on 17,806–17,942 fitted directions: n_i is a mean of ~560 squared projections, relative standard error ≈ 6 %, adequate per direction; but N itself is rank ≤ 563 and is used only through its diagonal in the eigenbasis (the mandate's construction). It makes the Wiener damping harsher and the floor higher than a pure-sampling N would, i.e. it works against the arms; c = 0.25 … 0.5 of the floor bracket that uncertainty and read null.
- **The eigenvalue floor (A′) was added after the first read of fold `orion_hct116`** with the v1 file (which had only the Wiener family); every fold was re-run with the frozen v2 file (`f885b93c…`, one sha for A, B and C). - **The construction is diagonal in the fitted eigenbasis.** A noise correction that re-estimates the eigenvectors (whiten by C − N, or by the out-of-sample covariance with corrected directions) is a different object; B's refits on half the cells are the nearest test of "different eigenvectors" this experiment ran.
