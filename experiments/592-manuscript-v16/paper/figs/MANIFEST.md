## F-2

**Caption.** The unit decomposition. Each row is one dataset and one experimental unit; ρ is that unit's agreement divided by the agreement of two halves of ONE construct's cells at the same budget — 1.0 means the unit costs nothing that more cells cannot buy back, 0 means the disagreement is irreducible. n is the number of targets (or target × line pairs) behind the ratio. Bars are the 95 % paired target bootstrap where `analyse285.json` (`rho`) stores one; the K562 guide row is the 748-perturbation cohort from `equiv285.json` (`frac`), which carries no stored interval but is the best-powered construct contrast on disk (the tight statistic there is the CELLS − GUIDE difference, +0.0058 [+0.0045, +0.0069]). The guide construct, not the batch, is the dominant irreducible term on CRISPRi libraries; the Cas9-knockout row is a modality hypothesis, not a controlled contrast.

**Sources.** `experiments/285-reliability-benchmark/work/analyse285.json (`rho`); .../equiv285.json (`frac`)`

**Caption.** Unit = h1-test targets (85; nmae is scored on 58), means over three emission seeds of 517's analytic pricer, which reproduces the production emitter's nmae to 5e-5 and fid to ~10 % (517 §1); open diamonds are real production-emitter runs (517 §9: mean-z hook nmae −0.0183, P_down hook −0.0149, the P_down hook at r 0.25 −0.0063).
