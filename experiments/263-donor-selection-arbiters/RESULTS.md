1. **The oracle headroom is real, large, and mostly on the anti-aligned group.** Replacing the equal-weight mean of the two whitened donor rows by the one with the higher cosine to the held truth lifts `pds` by **+0.0459** (3 cancer folds, norm-matched; +0.0490 / +0.0468 / +0.0417; as-is +0.0461) — 3.7× the deflation's +0.0123. 65 % of it sits on 262's group (e) (base·truth ≤ 0: **+0.17 per target** on 149), 35 % on the marginal band (+0.024 per target on 564), 0 % above 0.05 (§3, table H). Gating to the targets whose un-whitened donor rows disagree (cosine < 0; 68 per fold) keeps **+0.0236**; gating at < 0.1 keeps essentially all of it (+0.0451 on 238). The abstaining oracle (best of {donor 1, donor 2, the mean}) reaches **+0.0517**: the mean IS the best of the three on 41–45 % of two-donor targets. Priced as a ceiling (norm-matched): **+0.0135 pessimistic / +0.0210 point** overall (§8.1) — 1.6–2.5× the leader gap.
2. **The selection must be made in the whitened space.** The same oracle choice by un-whitened cosine to the truth captures 44 % (+0.0202); the whitened donor rows disagree with each other far more than the raw ones (median donor-donor cosine +0.018…+0.029 raw vs +0.007…+0.008 whitened), so "which donor is right for this line" is a question about the whitened rows.
3. **No observable arbiter finds it.** Seven arbiters (the CD4 row un-deflated and deflated, the H1-pair union row, KOLF2's own row on the cancer folds, donor row norm, kNN self-consistency, basal-profile similarity) × two spaces × three gatings = 78 selection cells: **every 3-fold mean is negative** (best `union:wht:tau0:nm` −0.0004 on 13 targets; typical −0.005 … −0.03), 69 of 78 at 0/3 folds, the other 9 at 1/3 (§4, table A). 4. **They are coin flips against the oracle's pick**: 44–61 % agreement on the cancer folds (n 72–259 per fold; the union's 60 % is on 72 targets), 27–44 % on `kolf2` (three-way, chance 33 %). Table G.

The best observable arbiter sits at 54–56 % on the same gates.
6. **The only positive cells on the cancer folds are reverse controls** (`cd4d:raw:tau0:rev:nm` +0.0018 at 1/3 folds, `union:raw:tau0:rev` +0.0016 at 3/3 — 1.1–1.2 σ; `extra263.log` §3): picking the donor the deflated CD4 row disagrees with is marginally better than picking the one it agrees with, which is 262 §6's finding (CD4·truth anti-correlates with the deflation's per-target Δ) seen from the selection side, and it is noise-sized.
7. **The as-is selection is amplitude-changing**: a single donor row carries 25–47 % more delivered amplitude than the mean of two disagreeing rows (delivered ratio 1.27–1.47 at gate all; `nmae89` +0.018 … +0.023), which is why every as-is cell is priced in the amplitude-changing class; the norm-matched variant (the selected row rescaled to the mean row's norm, `pds` unchanged within 0.001) is shape-only and is the reading used throughout.
8. **`kolf2` apart, one positive**: the production-realisable **three-donor majority rule** (keep the two un-whitened rows that agree, drop the odd one out) reads **+0.0121** `pds` on that fold (240 targets; leave-top-3-out retention 60 %; gainers 112 / losers 105; reverse −0.0097, random −0.0049; shape-only at delivered 0.9986, `cos_agg` +2.4 %, `nmae89` −0.0011). Priced on one fold, shape-only: +0.0051 pessimistic / +0.0076 point (mandate's factors). **One fold, an iPSC truth, reported apart; it cannot satisfy the 3/3 condition because the cancer folds have two donors** (§7).

## 1. Instrument and construction (`lib263.py`, `sweep263.py`; nothing mathematical is new downstream of the base)

162's PLOLO, three cancer folds (`k562_gwps` 269 targets, donors `orion_hct116` + `orion_hek293t`, operator `held_k562_gwps` `13d988fc…`; `orion_hct116` 290, `k562_gwps_pm50` + `orion_hek293t`, `7cecbf7f…`; `orion_hek293t` 294, `k562_gwps_pm50` + `orion_hct116`, `97e486e0…`) and `kolf2` (273, all three production donors, the production operator `e2f65ecf…`) reported apart. **The base is rebuilt from its parts**: each fold's per-donor prepared rows exactly as `plolo.build_fold` makes them (`E062.prepare_main_source`, the PerturbMatch 0.5 blend on the K562 rows' shared columns; KA-0 proves the rebuilt mean is the cached `pre` bit for bit), so a per-target selection or weighting is a combination of the donors' un-whitened rows — targets the arbiter does not touch keep the cached mean row verbatim — whitened by the fold's operator through `Operator.apply` (linear on the operator columns, ONE global preserve), then **261's `cd4svd5_parts` re-solved against the arm's own base**: CD4 deflated k = 5 (`lib246.deflate`), re-solved at f = 0.34 by `plolo.solve_fraction`; the H1-pair union re-solved by 218's carrying-row rule at g = 0.28; one add, one preserve (`lib246.compose`); scored by `lib246.score` (a23 + a15 tags; the scorer's per-target rank statistic). 188's exact `nmae` identity (wald89 pool, frame A) and the delivered norm through `lib257.Identity`, as 257–261.

**Norm matching.** A single donor row has a larger norm than the mean of two disagreeing rows, so the as-is selection raises the base's median row norm on the operator columns, the global preserve follows it, and the delivered amplitude moves by +7 … +47 % (table A, "deliv. ratio"). Every cell is therefore run twice: as-is, and **norm-matched** (`:nm`, the combined row rescaled to the cached mean row's norm before whitening), which isolates the direction effect — `pds` is scale-invariant per row and moves by < 0.001 between the two; `cos_agg`, `nmae` and the amplitude leg do not.

**Gating** τ ∈ {0, 0.1, always}: a selection applies only to targets carried by ≥ 2 donors whose un-whitened rows' cosine on the operator columns is < τ (`kolf2`: the minimum pairwise cosine): 84 / 59 / 61 / 138 targets at τ 0; 214 / 254 / 246 / 261 at τ 0.1; 259 / 259 / 259 / 268 always. **Arbiters** (score per donor j; pick = argmax; reverse = argmin; `raw` = the arbiter row against the donors' un-whitened rows on the operator columns, `wht` = against the same rows whitened alone): `cd4` cos(un-deflated CD4 row, donor row); `cd4d` the deflated CD4 row; `union` cos(H1-pair union row, donor row), carried targets only (72 / fold); `kolf2` cos(KOLF2's own prepared row, donor row) on the cancer folds (240 / fold; KOLF2 is on disk, never the truth there, not a production donor); `norm` the donor's row norm; `selfcons` kNN (k = 5) self-consistency — the mean cosine of donor j's row for t to j's rows for t's five nearest targets in the OTHER donor's space; `basal` the Pearson of the donor's control profile with the held line's on the operator columns (fold-constant: K562 0.823 vs HEK293T 0.798 on the K562 fold; 0.807 vs HCT116 0.869; 0.781 vs 0.869; on `kolf2` −0.091 / +0.074 / +0.082). On `kolf2` (three donors) `majority`: keep the two donors that agree (the highest mean cosine to the others), drop the odd one. **Softmax** w ∝ exp(β z), z the per-target-centred score over the SD of the centred scores, β ∈ {1, 3}, gate all. **Controls** per gate: random pick (3 seeds), the reverse of every arbiter, the mean (= `cd4svd5`). **Oracles**: `oracle_wht` the donor whose whitened row has the higher cosine to the held truth (scored columns); `oracle_raw` by un-whitened cosine; `oracle3_wht` the best of {donor rows, the mean row}.

## 10. Honest limits

- **One instrument, three cancer lines, an iPSC line apart.** The oracle is 162's PLOLO with deep pseudobulk truths on held operators; its +0.046 is a ceiling on that instrument and its transfer factor to the board for a base re-direction is unmeasured (the mandate's re-shape / re-dose bands were used). The verdict does not depend on it: every arbiter is negative before any factor is applied.
- **The arbiter list is what is on disk.** Seven observables, two spaces, three gatings, two weightings; combinations were not screened because every member is at chance and an ensemble of coins is a coin. A learned selector (a model of "which donor line this target's response follows", conditioned on the held line's basal state) is the only candidate left and is outside a screen.
- **The `kolf2` majority reading is one fold** on a non-cancer truth with a base at 0.64; its reverse and random controls are clean and the global-weight decomposition rules out the trivial explanation, but the rule loses in the whitened space and there is no second three-donor fold. The instrument that would test it — three-donor cancer folds, e.g. `kolf2` promoted to a whitened donor with re-fitted held operators — does not exist and would be a new experiment, not this one.
- **Norm matching is my construction**, not the builder's: it makes the selection shape-only on PLOLO; a production form would have to reproduce it (or re-solve α) and be checked on the delivered amplitude.
- **Retention is undefined on negative sums**; reported as computed (> 100 %) and not used.
- **Peak RSS** 5.2–5.5 GiB per fold process with the operator resident, two processes at a time (10.9 GiB together, 64 GiB machine).
