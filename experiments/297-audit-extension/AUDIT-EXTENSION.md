## 0. What changed my priors while doing this

Three things, all of which weaken a headline the programme currently holds:

1. **F13's surviving sign is published, in the shape a reviewer will recognise.** Wang et al. 2026 score every
   perturbation on two axes — split-half reliability ρ and **φ, alignment with "the transcriptional axis common"**
   across perturbations — and label φ ≥ 0.5 *shared*, the rest *specific*. They then report that
   *"in 39 of 41 method–benchmark combinations, mean model performance followed Shared > Specific > Unreliable"*
   [WANG26, **[F]**]. That is our F13 sign — generic responses are the transferable ones — measured on 7,170
   perturbations across 29 datasets, with prediction performance as the outcome. F13's *target* (cross-line
   conservation of the response direction) is a different quantity, and 290's design is much stronger, but the
   direction is not ours to claim.
2. **The breadth half of F13 is sitting in a published supplementary table our own audit already read.** In the CD4
   source's K562-comparison table, cross-cell-type conservation tracks the MASH DE-gene count at ρ **+0.507 / +0.530 /
   +0.543** (287's CHECK 4, re-read today as KA-297). "Broad responses are more conserved" is therefore already
   visible, at a *larger* correlation than F13's out-of-fold model achieves on its own target.
3. **F14's guide term for CD4 is literally the source paper's own column.** `guide_correlation_all` medians
   +0.0617 / +0.0715 / +0.0607 [GWCD4i-TAB, **[287]**/KA-297] against `guide_correlation_signif` +0.600 / +0.622 /
   +0.643. Our ρ_guide for CD4 is **0.02–0.06** — the same number, from the same screen. And the field already treats
   guide efficacy as the dominant nuisance: Mixscale exists to weight cells by knockdown strength [MIXSCALE, **[S]**],
   and a 2025 re-analysis reports that in published CRISPRi screens only ≈ 97/240 (iPSC) and ≈ 56/377 (K562) sgRNAs
   pass a stringent efficacy criterion [ZHANG25-CRISPRi, **[S]**, unverified against the source text].

## 2. What the write-up can now say — item by item

**Item 1 (F13).** Say: *"On a source-disjoint, annotation-orthogonal target we reproduce, out of fold and at the
measurement ceiling, the published direction that responses aligned with the shared transcriptional axis are the
transferable ones (Wang et al. 2026), extend it from model performance to cross-line conservation, and show by
ablation that pathway identity contributes ≈ 8 % over random matched gene sets."* Do **not** say the sign is new, and do
not present the +0.39 margin over annotation (a property of 290's target, withdrawn off-map by 291). State that the CD4
source's own table already shows breadth→conservation at ρ +0.53. The preregistered refutation of our own opposite
prediction belongs in the methods as a discipline point, not in the abstract as a finding.

**Item 2 (F14).** Say: *"We place five units of variance — cells, UMI depth, batch, guide construct, study, line, state,
donor — on one matched design and one statistic, verify Wang et al.'s reliability law on four real cell ladders, and
report that a different study in the same cell line agrees better than a second guide construct in the same study."*
The new sentence is the study-vs-construct inversion (2e); everything else is harmonisation and re-measurement and must
credit [GWCD4i-TAB], [REPLOGLE22] and [WANG26] by name. Drop any phrasing that implies we discovered the guide term.
The cells-vs-depth equivalence must name the objective (pseudobulk response reproducibility) in the same sentence,
because the budget literature's headline for other objectives is the opposite.

**Item 3 (293).** Say: *"On the challenge's own 2025 library, two-guide agreement splits cleanly on knockdown success
(+0.687 vs −0.003), the two-construct targets are the library's weakest by design (P = 0.0015) so any library-wide
two-guide statistic is a lower bound, and the resulting construct-level ceiling on a zero-shot prediction is cos
0.21–0.57 — well above what any current entry achieves."* The mechanism is the field's, cite it. The publishable ideas
are the **selection effect** on two-construct targets and the **construct-level vs cell-split ceiling** distinction.
Delete or downgrade "roughly one truth in five is a response to the vector": it rests on two constructs and the only
characterised mechanism is reported at 0.8–4.7 % of guides.

**Item 4 (284).** Say: *"After 21 annotation features, the per-gene remainder of a cross-line conservation map
reproduces across two source halves that share no source and no cell line (ρ +0.328 against a ceiling of +0.382,
permutation null +0.044), so cross-context behaviour is a gene property beyond family and essentiality that annotation
does not carry."* This is the cleanest new scientific statement of the five items and it should be stated with its
withdrawal of F12b attached. Label the described tail as description.

**Item 5 (286).** Say: *"Rank retrieval on this operator is paid in directions where an unbiased cross-product estimate
of the signal covariance finds no signal, and the effect survives re-estimating the basis from independent views."*
Cite Jolliffe (1982) and the whitening literature's task-dependence in the same paragraph, and state that our objective
is a 300-way scale-invariant rank retrieval, not classification or semantic similarity. Concede that an undetectable
signal at n/p ≈ 1.6 is not an absent one, and that the estimator class is itself known to be biased (PNAS 2025).

**Item 6 (map off-map, C5).** Say: *"A conservation score frozen on 28 sources predicts per-gene transfer into eight
