# The Lab (800–899): a separate research effort, verifier first

Written 2026-10-16 by the production coordinator ([2d6f46]) at Ahmet's request ("no need to rush… let's do them properly"; keep it
separate from the four production sessions so the group does not grow). This file is the lab's charter. The lab lead keeps its own
log in `docs/LAB-LOG.md` and its experiments in `experiments/8NN-*`.

## Why a lab, and why verifier first
- The production team spends its days on slots, plumbing and small riders (+0.0004 … +0.0013). The gap to the top of the A/B/C board is
  0.03–0.2, and the prize is on three unseen lines with a dark board. Ideas that could close part of that gap need slow, careful work.
- Agent swarms work in mathematics because the checker (Lean) is exact, instant and free. Ours is a held-out test on public data that is
  noisy (± ~0.001 overall), slow (minutes to hours, 8–25 GB per job, one at a time on the Mac) and wears out: every idea tried on the
  same folds makes a lucky false winner more likely. On 10-15 it over-read PIE by ~+0.004 (702 HARM on the board).
- So the lab's first product is a better checker, and only then a set of idea agents. More agents without a stronger checker produce
  plausible ideas we cannot tell apart.

## Phase A — the checker (first, ~2 days; do it properly)
1. **One command, cloud-parallel:** price a candidate (a bulk-dump transform, a DE/emission change, or a whole new model's predictions)
   on N held-out folds on EC2, return member-level numbers in board units with target-bootstrap CIs and the try count. Build on what
   exists: `scripts/cloud_score.sh` (real cell-eval2 on a self-terminating worker, ~$1.50 a run), 522's fold dumps (`dump522.py`),
   708's `dump708.py` / `score708.py`, 438's `dayof438.py`, the h1-test and 618 real-emitter surfaces, science's `cloud700.sh`.
2. **Validate the validator (AGENTS 11.1 rule 3):** before any idea is priced, the checker must retrodict known board reads in sign and
   rough size: 495 (cap 6, +0.0016), 498p2g (+0.0010), 446bj2z (zs), 496a (hook), DN (PARTIAL, +0.0004), 702 (PIE bulk, HARM −0.0025),
   479sp2 (FAIL). Report its hit rate and its over-read ratio; a checker that would have passed 702 is not trusted yet.
3. **Fresh, sealed test sets:** build new held-out folds from public data nobody has tuned on (new Arc-like 300-target panel draws by
   AGENTS §2.1's selection rules, lines left out of every donor mix). Split them into a WORKING set the idea agents may use and a SEALED
   reserve that only the lab lead opens, once, for a final candidate (record it in `docs/CONFIRMATION-LEDGER.md` the way R6/R7 were).
4. **Wear accounting:** every evaluation logs (surface, candidate, config); the checker reports how many configs each surface has seen,
   and applies 11.1c.4's best-of-N correction to any "winner".

## Phase B — idea agents (after the checker passes its known answers)
Three or four agents, each with its own territory (no two on the same question), plus one judge:
- **Direction on strong targets** (706: the strong-target gap is direction, not amplitude; 707 is the first lead).
- **A genuinely new model:** trained across all public Perturb-seq we hold (data/external: Replogle K562/RPE1, Nadig HepG2/Jurkat,
  Orion HCT116/HEK293T, Jiang lines, VCC25 H1, Tahoe…), always evaluated leave-one-line-out. The long-term lane.
- **The DE channel** (fid 0.005 vs the field's 0.04–0.09; 721: the lever is sign accuracy on called non-DE genes, closed for every
  public source tried; per-target call-set sizing closed by payoff unless n_real becomes predictable).
- **Data:** any public line or screen we lack (the Pisces watch, 2026 releases, supplements).
- **The judge:** reads every proposal before it gets compute; kills the weak, asks for the prediction and the mechanism first.
Every idea is preregistered (prediction, bins, class: transfer / scorer mechanics / A/B/C-specific) before its numbers exist.

## Boundaries (the lab must never break production)
- **Writes only** in `experiments/8NN-*`, `docs/LAB-*.md`; commits with a pathspec (`git commit -m … -- <paths>`); pushes allowed.
- **Never touches** the builder, `def_day.sh`, `october_recipe.py`, `docs/october-decisions.json`, waiters, slots, `vcc`, the shared
  docs (FINDINGS, NEXT-SLOT-DECISION, BOARD-LEDGER, PLAN), or the production sessions' experiments.
- **Machine:** heavy compute on EC2, not the Mac. Local jobs ≤ 8 GB, only through `experiments/602-council-gate/gate602.sh`, never beside
  a production build. Local disk ≤ 30 GB in total; keep ≥ 60 GiB free (`df -h /System/Volumes/Data`).
- **Cloud budget:** ≤ $800 through Nov 5 (Ahmet has ~$2,000 of AWS credits). Every instance: on-demand for jobs > 30 min,
  terminate-on-shutdown, a dead-man `shutdown -h` timer, `scripts/aws_reap.sh` after each batch; log every launch with its cost in
  `docs/LAB-LOG.md`. The second computer (16 GB) is not a compute node.
- **Agents:** Claude Code (Ahmet's Anthropic credits), newest model, high effort; never Sonnet. Workers via `scripts/claude_subagent.sh`
  or separate sessions, each with a written mandate in its experiment dir; launch long jobs with `scripts/detach.sh`.

## Interface to production (the only one)
- A finished candidate becomes a **dossier** in `experiments/8NN-*/DOSSIER.md`: mechanism and class, the checker's price on fresh
  folds with CI and try count, its known-answer record, and an UNAPPLIED implementation (a dump transform script or a patch with its KA).
- The lab lead sends the production coordinator ONE message per dossier. The coordinator decides whether it gets an A/B/C slot
  (last A/B/C slot 10-21) or, after Oct 22, a replacement final entry under OCT22-CHECKLIST §C 6a (one attempt, none after 11-04 12:00Z).
- Nothing reaches the October recipe without both an A/B/C board read (or a written reason) and the lab's held-out read (AGENTS 11.1c.2).
