## Datasets used in training / fitting
- **Public CRISPR perturbation screens (single-cell):**
  - Replogle 2022: K562 genome-wide, K562 essential, RPE1 essential.
  - Nadig 2025: HepG2, Jurkat.
  - Xaira X-Atlas/Orion: HCT116, HEK293T.
  - Pan 2025: H1 hESC, GSE295214.
  - The 2025 Virtual Cell Challenge H1 data.
  - A genome-wide CRISPRi screen in primary CD4 T cells.
  - A DLD-1 low-MOI screen.
  - Jiang 2025: A549, MCF7, HT29, HAP1, BxPC3, K562.
  - Sunshine 2023 (Calu-3), Wessels (THP-1), Myllia (A549).
  - VIPerturb-seq K562 was evaluated and not used.
- **Basal expression:** CCLE / DepMap expression, to recognise a context's cell line from its control cells.
- **The challenge's own non-targeting control cells** for each context, for conditioning and calibration. No perturbed
  challenge data exists to train on.

## Data processing and model inputs
- Each screen is reduced to per-target pseudobulk response rows (log-scale difference from its own controls) on the
  challenge's gene axis.
- Per-gene "programme" statistics (the fraction of a screen's perturbations that lower each gene) are computed from the
  same tables.
- The context's controls provide its library-size distribution, per-gene expression and detectability, and the
  jackknife noise level used for calibration.

## The model
A fitted **donor-transfer model**: a regression of the target context's response on public donors' responses. Components,
all fitted on held-out lines (leave-one-line-out folds over the public screens) and on the 2025 H1 data, then checked on
the validation leaderboard:
1. **Transfer operator.** A whitening operator fitted on the donor signal covariance (shrinkage γ 0.65) maps donor
   response rows into a space where cross-line structure transfers.
2. **Composition.**
   - Per-target donor weights: a norm-relative cap.
   - A primary-T-cell residual with low-rank deflation.
   - An H1 residual union.
   - Composition weights re-priced for the final panel.
3. **Per-target magnitude.** A cross-donor shared-energy key: the agreement of independent donors on a target's
   response, clipped, sets each target's effect size.
4. **Shape.** Magnitude sharpening sign(h)|h|^q (q 1.75), with row norms preserved.
5. **Per-context calibration.** The effect scale is levelled per context against a noise-calibrated target computed from
   that context's own non-targeting controls: an EV-optimal point under the profile-error metric's asymmetric loss.
6. **Donor weighting by cell identity.**
   - When a context's control profile identifies a line (a unanimous CCLE match) for which a public screen exists, that
     screen gets a higher donor weight (w 2 for strong screens, 0.5 for weak ones) for the targets it covers.
   - Its shared "programme" is not used: it proved lab-specific.
   - The weight was fitted on H1 with an independent same-line screen.
7. **Differential-expression layer.**
   - Call-set prior: control detectability plus the strongest predicted genes.
   - Sign prior: a cross-line consensus of the fraction of perturbations that lower each gene, with the strongest 20 %
     protected.
   - A fold-change floor at the minimum detectable effect (the L1-optimal magnitude under sign uncertainty).
- **Architecture:** no neural network. Linear-algebraic transfer with fitted hyperparameters, chosen by held-out-line and
  leaderboard validation.

## Classical / non-learned components, including anything blended with model outputs (disclosed in full)
- **Emission to counts (a rendering step).**
  - Predictions are rendered as 400 integer-count cells per perturbation. Per-cell library classes follow the context's
    control quantiles.
  - A few very deep "ballast" cells per perturbation (≤ 1,000,000 counts each, within the format's cap) carry the
    predicted pseudobulk composition. The other cells carry the differential-expression design.
  - This lets the profile-level and the DE-level predictions both be expressed in one count matrix.
- **A zero-gene repair:** gated genes whose rendered total would be zero get a floor.
- **Same-line donors (component 6):** public screens of the recognised line enter as donors inside the transfer model.
  Their rows are blended with the model's direction at the model's own magnitude, then rendered by the model.
  Published measurements are never submitted as predictions (Arc's clarification of 2026-09-17).
- No challenge perturbation data, lab results or literature values are used to revise outputs. Everything submitted is
  generated by the fitted model and its renderer.

## Training, adaptation and inference
- Fitting: operator and weights on public screens, with hyperparameters chosen by leave-one-line-out folds and the 2025
  H1 data.
- Adaptation per context at inference: identity recognition, per-context scale levelling from the context's controls,
  same-line donor weighting.
- Inference: one deterministic pipeline (`def_day.sh`) from the released controls to the submission file, rehearsed end
  to end on held-out bundles and once, blind, on the validation lines (experiment 453).

## Compute
One laptop (Apple M5 Max, 64 GB) for everything, plus a few disposable cloud CPU instances for offline scoring. No GPUs.
