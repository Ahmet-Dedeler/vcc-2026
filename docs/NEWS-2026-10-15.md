## 3. PIE (Arc, 2026-10-05)

- Post: https://arcinstitute.org/news/pie · Preprint: bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.10.02.756297 (Verma, Adduri,
  Bevilacqua, Eraslan, Burke, Goodarzi, Roohani; Arc), PDF + extracted text in
  `experiments/680-news-intel-1015/work/papers/` · Code: github.com/ArcInstitute/pie (48 stars, created 09-29,
  v1.0.0 10-05, PyPI `arc-pie` 1.0.0, Python 3.12, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for code) · HF collection `arcinstitute/pie`.
- What it predicts: for each (context, perturbation, gene): `p_de` (probability DE), `lfc_pred`, `delta_p_pred`
  (change in mean on the preprocessed scale). Inputs: knowledge-source embeddings (ESM2, NCBI text, STRING,
  DepMap gene effect, Cellosaurus context text, perturbation text, ...), the context's control mean, and a
  "response evidence" vector (per-gene mean/sd of pseudobulk delta and LFC over training responses to the same
  perturbation, plus same-context evidence; empty for unseen perturbations/contexts).
- Checkpoint `PIE_replogle_xdataset` (`best_auprc.ckpt`, 966 MB): trained on Tahoe-100M, Jiang (GSE281048,
  461 conditions), X-Atlas/Orion (13,284 conditions), Arc VCC 2025 H1 (190 conditions); evaluated zero-shot on
  Replogle–Nadig (6,642 genes). Reported on the four held-out lines (`eval/test_unseen/metrics_best_auprc.csv`):
  binary_auprc 0.246 (hepg2 .227, jurkat .210, k562 .218, rpe1 .330), sig_jaccard 0.120, direction_match 0.647,
  spearman_lfc 0.270. Within-dataset unseen-context AUPRC 0.424 vs Rhaister-O 0.344 (strongest baseline);
  baselines named in the preprint: State, Stack, Rhaister-O (unseen contexts), COMPASS-N, PRESAGE, TabICL
  (unseen perturbations).
- DE labels in training were called with gpudge: Wilcoxon per (perturbation, gene), 5-CPM filter, BH per
  perturbation — the same recipe as cell-eval2's DE channel.
- Licence (`PIE_MODEL_LICENSE.md`, `PIE_AUP.md` saved): model and derivatives non-commercial; "Non-Commercial
  Entity" includes a natural person not acting for a Commercial Entity; "Non-Commercial Purpose" excludes "direct
  or indirect monetary compensation"; the AUP says "you are free to use any outputs you create for any purpose"
  and "Outputs (as defined in the License) are not subject to this Section 1". Redistribution of outputs needs
  the citation. Judgement: a solo personal-capacity entrant using PIE outputs as one input is inside the
  letter; the FAQ's explicit State/Stack carve-out does not mention PIE, so the clean move for the write-up is to
  cite it as a "published model used directly or adapted" (the rules require listing it anyway).
- On the board: ~25 model names contain PIE/pie; best 404NotFound "PIE_m_Mag080cM" 0.2467, then magpie 0.1894,
  Dandelion "PIE_SUPPORT_OCT14_S1" 0.1608, "E_primary_pie_v2" (Q Zhao, −0.0085 on 10-08). No top-20 team names it.
- Arc said nothing linking PIE to the 2026 challenge: not in the post, preprint (VCC 2025 appears only as a
  training set), repo (0 issues), rules or FAQ.
